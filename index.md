# Privacy Policy for Walker

Last updated: September 2026

Walker ("the App") is built with complete user privacy in mind.

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
Walker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. All data generated or processed by the App—including sensor information and workouts—remains strictly on your local Apple Watch and paired iOS device. 

### 2. HealthKit and Sensor Data
The App reads sensor and HealthKit workout data solely to provide real-time metrics during active sessions. This data is processed exclusively on-device. It is never transmitted across the internet, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or tracking purposes.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
